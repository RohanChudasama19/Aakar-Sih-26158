Your first reconstruction

  1. Start the worker. Use the Docker instructions in README.md, or start the local CPU server. Worker status appears above your missions.
  2. Try the sample. Create a mission and click “Use bundled sample files.” This loads a synthetic 6-second video with matching GPS and metadata.
  3. Reconstruct. Select CPU for the smoke test. Follow stages A–F. Upload progress and reconstruction progress are shown separately.
  4. Explore the result. Rotate, zoom and pan. Use distance or area mode to select points on the model. GPS-aligned does not mean surveyed accuracy.
  5. Export. Download the complete bundle to keep model textures and supporting files together.
For a real mission, use steady forward motion and changing viewpoints. Avoid pure rotation, severe blur, water, glass and featureless ground. A nearly straight GPS track may leave absolute orientation unresolved.
